## Design kit
Tone: quiet, precise, architectural, editorial, warm-neutral, human, clinical, modern, controlled. Not SaaS/template/AI/sci-fi/hotel/salon/crypto/generic health.
Colors: --bg-main #F4F4F1; --bg-soft #F8F8F5; --surface #FCFCFA; --surface-muted #ECECE7; --text-primary #151515; --text-secondary #696965; --text-faint rgba(20,20,20,.20); --border-soft rgba(20,20,20,.10); --black #111; --white #fff. No cyan/neon/purple/blue gradients, gradient text, colored cards. Colour comes from photography.
**Desktop cinematic stage** (reference): centered stage width 90–91.5vw, max-width 1310–1330px, margin-inline auto; major scenes' visible stage ≈ 610–650px tall at 1440×1080 (not forced identical). Stage radius 8–14px (not rounded-3xl everywhere).
**Ambient outer layer**: outside the stage, the active/related image enlarged (~scale 1.25), blur ~60px, saturate ~.65, opacity ~.12, edges dissolving into warm white — colour spill, not a visible bg image. Use for Hero, Benefits, Doctors, Results (slightly warmer: lips/skin tones), Booking only.
Type (Inter Tight kept): hero clamp(52px,4.4vw,64px) lh .94 ls -.045em w500 (NOT 100px); section headings 46–58px; active treatment heading 34–44px; body 15–18px lh 1.45–1.6; labels 10–12px ls .08–.14em. Premium via whitespace/position/rhythm/crop, not size. No 800/900.

## Motion rules
Tokens: --ease-primary cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1); --ease-soft cubic-bezier(.33,1,.68,1); --duration-fast 220ms; --duration-ui 320ms; --duration-medium 650ms; --duration-large 1050ms. No elastic/spring/bounce/overshoot/idle floating. Every large animation must change a spatial relationship (image expands in stage, panel rises over scene, cards move while stage pinned, active row gains space, stable stage changes state, portrait masks over portrait, previous scene reveals next). "Element fades in" alone does not deserve a major animation.
Intensity: Hero HIGH · Intro LOW–MED · Benefits HIGH · Treatments MED–HIGH · Journey HIGH · Doctors MED · Results MED · FAQ LOW · Booking MED · Footer VERY LOW. Rhythm: cinematic→quiet→cinematic→interactive→cinematic→human→interactive→quiet→finale→quiet.
Lenis: audit existing, single instance, synced with gsap.ticker + ScrollTrigger; no lag/resistance. Cleanup triggers; no DOM geometry reads on raw scroll; transform/opacity/clip-path.
