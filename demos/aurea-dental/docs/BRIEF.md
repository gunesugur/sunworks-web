# AUREA Dental — Design & Motion Brief (condensed from client spec)

Premium European dental clinic × editorial design studio × architecture. Calm, clinical, precise, human, trustworthy, technologically advanced. NOT corporate, template, sci-fi, playful, luxurious-for-show, AI-generated looking.
**Motion is part of the layout**: scroll choreography, masking, image expansion, pinned scenes, controlled horizontal movement, timed type reveals. One continuous film, not independent sections. Site must still look premium with all motion off.

## Visual system
- Palette (near-monochrome): bg #F4F4F1 / #F8F8F6, white #FCFCFA, surface #ECECE8, border rgba(20,20,20,.10), text #141414, text-2 #686864, CTA #111 / text #FFF (hover #242424). Very subtle warm beige/metal-grey accent only in tiny states.
- Forbidden: blue/cyan/turquoise medical gradients, tooth icons/logos, stock smiles, purple gradients, blobs, glassmorphism everywhere, neon/glow, bento grids, gradient text, mesh bgs, particles, excessive pills/rounded cards, drop shadows, badge spam, fake charts, marquee, 4 identical icon cards, alternating image/text rhythm, centered hero+2 buttons everywhere.
- Layout: max width 1440–1560; gutters 48–72 desktop, 80–96 large, 32 tablet, 18–24 mobile. Hidden 12-col grid. Vary compositions: full-bleed, inset, asymmetric, 40/60, 55/45, pinned, horizontal overflow. Lots of negative space, thin structural lines, small editorial labels, oversized controlled type, large rectangular images, soft radius (not pills), small circular buttons.
- Type: Inter Tight variable. Hero clamp(56px,6vw,96px), lh .92–1, weight 450–550, ls -0.045em. Section titles 48–72. Body 16–19 / 1.45–1.6. Labels 10–12 uppercase ls .08–.14em. Buttons 13–14. Never 800/900.

## Motion tokens (use only these)
--ease-primary cubic-bezier(0.22,1,0.36,1); --ease-soft cubic-bezier(0.33,1,0.68,1); --duration-fast 220ms; --duration-ui 320ms; --duration-medium 650ms; --duration-large 1100ms. Micro 180–300, nav/buttons 250–350, reveal 500–800, image expansion 900–1400, section transition 800–1300, big choreography = scrubbed by scroll. No bounce/overshoot/elastic. Animate transform/opacity/clip-path; avoid width/height/top/left during scroll. Lenis smooth scroll synced to GSAP ticker + ScrollTrigger; user stays in control. prefers-reduced-motion: no pinning/parallax, opacity-only, all content usable.
Motion intensity: Hero HIGH, Intro LOW, Benefits HIGH, Services MED-HIGH, Journey HIGH, Doctors MED, Results MED, FAQ LOW, Booking MED, Footer VERY LOW. Rhythm alternates cinematic / quiet.
**≥5 connected section transitions** (hero compresses as intro enters; benefits bg stays while services panel rises over; services → journey; journey stage slides beneath doctor panel; doctor → results; FAQ recedes as booking portrait rises).

## Micro interactions
Buttons: text shifts 2px left, arrow circle expands slightly, arrow translateX 0→3px, bg #111→#242424, no scale. Links: underline grows left→right. Circular controls hover scale 1.08 / 220ms. Interactive images scale 1→1.025 over 800ms inside overflow hidden. No giant custom cursors.

## Copy tone
Clear, calm, human, informative. No "Unlock your dream smile" / "Revolutionizing" / "Where technology meets excellence". Prefer e.g. "Digital scans help us plan treatment with greater accuracy and explain each step before treatment begins." Trust = evidence and clarity (specialists, digital scanning, 3D planning, transparent plans, sterilization, aftercare), never "#1 / award winning".

## Accessibility & performance
WCAG contrast, keyboard for everything, visible focus, nothing hover-only, ARIA accordions, real labels, semantic HTML. transform/opacity, responsive AVIF/WebP, lazy below fold, preload only hero assets.
